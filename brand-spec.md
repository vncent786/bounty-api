## Product position

Bounty turns online conversations into cited findings users can research, save, monitor, and apply. “Horizontal” is an internal product architecture decision, not customer-facing positioning.

## Interface system

- Anchor: editorial research desk
- Background: `#0b0c0d`
- Primary surface: `#111315`
- Raised surface: `#171a1d`
- Rules: `#2a2e31`
- Primary text: `#ece9e2`
- Secondary text: `#9b9d9d`
- Muted text: `#6f7374`
- Focus/link: `#77a7c7`
- Confirmed evidence: `#77a681`
- Uncertain/partial: `#c2a467`
- Failure/unavailable: `#bd7973`
- UI/display font: Instrument Sans
- Evidence/provenance font: IBM Plex Mono
- Spacing: 4px base grid
- Radius: 3px for data surfaces, 5px for controls, 8px for dialogs
- Elevation: flat by default; shadow only for overlays
- Motion: 120–160ms opacity and color; no hover translation

## Copy rules

- Lead with findings, evidence, and next actions.
- Do not use “horizontal,” “ontology,” “candidate intelligence,” or internal pipeline terminology in user-facing copy.
- Never claim a broad pattern from one voice.
- Show missing, partial, unavailable, and not-yet-run states explicitly.
- Never display fabricated examples or sample metrics as real data.
