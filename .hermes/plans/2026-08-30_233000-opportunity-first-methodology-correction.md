# Opportunity-first methodology correction release contract

## Primary loop
A fresh owned-worker scan preserves valid social evidence from the last 90 days, surfaces every specific cited firsthand observation as an Opportunity Investigation unless it fails an integrity check, reserves capacity for depth/history on shortlisted topics, and publishes the completed persisted run to the production snapshot without making new Google or model calls.

## Release blockers
- Hidden age deletion below 90 days.
- Opportunity rejection caused only by trade-stage anomaly, parity, persistence, breadth, depth, or company-investigability gates.
- Broad adaptive fan-out exhausting all history/depth capacity before candidate qualification.
- Snapshot generation re-fetching Google or model data already persisted by the scan.
- Production continuing to display an older run after a successful snapshot build.

## Trade gates unchanged
Specificity, independent behavior, firsthand quality, persistence, breadth, comparable anomaly, parity, conversation-depth completeness, and investigability remain strict for trade-ready promotion.

## Excluded
- Forcing a trade-ready result.
- Fabricating missing history or citations.
- Country-level Google expansion beyond persisted data.
- Paid data vendors.

## Acceptance
- 90-day and 91-day boundary regressions.
- Latest eight-candidate fixture produces more than two cited Opportunity Investigations without increasing trade-ready count.
- Adaptive plan is globally bounded and leaves room for 8 x 4 historical windows.
- Snapshot-builder test proves zero external Google/model calls.
- Full suite, exact-tree independent review, fresh bounded scan, snapshot publish, production desktop/mobile verification.
